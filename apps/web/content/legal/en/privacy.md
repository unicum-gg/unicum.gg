## Who is responsible

The data controller is {publisher}, the publisher of {name}, whose details are on the [legal notice]({company-page}). Every question about your data goes to [{email}](mailto:{email}).

## World of Tanks data

Most of what {name} shows is not collected from you. It is the public data Wargaming publishes about every account and clan through its official API, which we read, store and compute over:

- an account’s nickname, clan, battle statistics, vehicles, medals and ratings, and a clan’s name, tag, members, description and results;
- how those figures change over time, including past nicknames and past clans, which is what lets the site draw progression and history;
- for players who link a Twitch channel, whether that channel is live.

We process it on the basis of our **legitimate interest** in publishing statistics about a public game, the same figures Wargaming itself shows. Wargaming’s own handling of your account is described in [its privacy policy]({wargaming-policy}).

## Your account

Signing in is optional, and only possible through Wargaming.net ID. Your password is typed on Wargaming’s own page and never reaches us. When you sign in, we keep:

- your Wargaming account ID, nickname and region, and the access token Wargaming returns, which is encrypted at rest and lets us read your own account data and act for you only where you set that up;
- a session cookie, with the IP address and browser that opened it, so the session stays yours;
- the Twitch or Discord accounts you choose to connect, identified by their ID and username, which you can disconnect from your settings at any time.

We process this to **provide the service you asked for**: an account, its settings and its connections.

## The game mod

The optional [game mod]({mod-page}) sends us what happens in the battles you play with it installed:

- the battle results the game itself shows after a battle, which name every participant, so a battle can appear on the profiles of players who never installed the mod;
- the equipment, consumables, shells, field modifications and crew skills fitted for each battle;
- a token linking the mod to your account, stored only as a hash.

From your settings you can hide your battle list from everyone but you, and take your loadouts off the tables that name players. Hidden loadouts still count, anonymously, in each vehicle’s figures.

## What you publish

Some features exist to publish what you write, and are read by a moderator first:

- your ratings and written reviews of vehicles and maps, shown with your nickname once approved;
- the videos you submit, with the battle details you give;
- messages sent through the feedback form, with your nickname when you are signed in, delivered to a private Discord channel.

## Supporter payments

Payments on the [support page]({support-page}) are handled entirely by Stripe. Your card details are typed on Stripe’s page and never reach us. See [Stripe’s privacy policy]({stripe-policy}).

We keep your Stripe customer identifier, the amount, currency, date and status of each payment, and whether a monthly pledge is active, so your supporter badge and Discord role follow it. Accounting records are kept for the period French law requires.

Supporters appear on the public supporters board with their nickname and total. You can choose to be hidden from it in your settings.

## Cookies and local storage

{name} stores a few things in your browser:

- **Essential:** the session cookie, and short-lived cookies that protect sign-in and account connections, without which those features cannot work.
- **Preferences:** your region, language, rating metric, display choices and recent searches, kept so the site opens the way you left it.
- **Audience measurement:** Umami, which counts visits without any cookie and without identifying you.
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies and is **only loaded if you accept it** in the cookie banner.

You can change your choice at any time with “Manage cookies” at the bottom of every page.

## Who else handles it

We do not sell your data, and there are no ads. These providers handle part of it for us, each under its own policy:

- **netcup** (Germany): hosting of the site, the database and the API.
- **Cloudflare**: delivery and protection of the site, which sees every request. [Policy]({cloudflare-policy})
- **Wargaming**: sign-in and game data. [Policy]({wargaming-policy})
- **Stripe**: payments. [Policy]({stripe-policy})
- **Discord**: moderation of reviews and videos, feedback, and the supporter role. [Policy]({discord-policy})
- **Twitch**: live status of linked channels. [Policy]({twitch-policy})
- **Umami** (EU-hosted): audience measurement. [Policy]({umami-policy})
- **Google**: Google Analytics 4, only with your consent. [Policy]({google-policy})

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers there rely on the safeguards the GDPR provides for, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- **World of Tanks data:** as part of the statistics history, for as long as the site publishes it.
- **Account and connections:** until you ask us to delete your account.
- **Sessions:** until you sign out or the session expires.
- **Payment records:** for the period French accounting law requires.
- **Ratings, reviews and videos:** as long as they are published, or until you withdraw them.
- **Server logs:** a few days at most.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access the data we hold about you, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Where processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Write to [{email}](mailto:{email}) from an address we can reply to, and tell us which account it concerns. We answer within one month.

We are glad to sort out any question about your data directly. You also have the right to contact the French data protection authority, the [CNIL]({cnil}).

This policy changes when the site does. The date at the top of the page says when it last did.
