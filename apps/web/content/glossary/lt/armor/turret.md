term: Tornado

A specifically designed platform for the gun, whose armor and rotation speed determine how the vehicle engages from cover.

A tornado allows the vehicle to aim without turning its hull, which keeps the front facing the enemy and minimizes dispersion. Its armor is usually the thickest part of the vehicle, as it is the section that is visible over a ridge.

Tornado-less vehicles sacrifice this for a larger gun or a smaller profile. They aim within a fixed arc and have to turn the entire hull for targets outside this range, which is slower and increases the size of the aiming circle.
