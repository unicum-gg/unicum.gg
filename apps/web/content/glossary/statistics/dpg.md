The average damage dealt per battle, the most quoted measure of personal output and the one most sensitive to which vehicles are played.

DPG is total damage divided by battles. On a single vehicle it is directly comparable between players, which is why it is the number quoted when someone claims a tank is good or bad in their hands.

Across an account it compares nothing on its own. A Tier X heavy deals two to three times the damage of a Tier V, so an account average mostly reports which tiers that player likes. Per-vehicle ratings exist to correct exactly this.

It also ignores everything that is not damage. A scout that spots for 4,000 damage and deals 800 reads as a bad game unless assistance is counted alongside it.
