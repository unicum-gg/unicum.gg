Vehicles destroyed for each one lost, which is kills per battle divided by how often the account dies.

It is the ratio a shooter player expects: everything destroyed, over every battle that ended in the garage. A player who kills one enemy per battle and survives half of them lands at two.

The number moves on two independent things, which is what makes it worth reading next to kills per battle rather than instead of it: finishing more targets raises it, and so does simply dying less. A cautious player who trades little can hold a high ratio on modest damage, and a scout who dies every battle by design will sit low whatever they contributed.
