Average vehicles destroyed per battle, a measure of finishing rather than of damage.

A kill is credited to whoever lands the final shot, so the statistic rewards being in position to finish targets. It correlates with damage without following it: a big-alpha vehicle that fires once a target is low collects kills that a fast-firing gun spread over the whole team would not.

The server average sits near one kill per battle by construction, since fifteen vehicles are destroyed across thirty players in a typical game. Anything above 1.3 is strong.
