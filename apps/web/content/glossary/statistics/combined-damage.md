Damage dealt plus assistance credited, the figure Marks of Excellence are measured on.

Combined damage adds what a player did directly to what they made possible: damage dealt, plus spotting and tracking assistance. It is the closest single number to a player's contribution to their team's damage output.

It is also what makes marking a light tank possible. On a scout, most of the contribution never appears in the damage column, and a measure that ignored assistance would put every light vehicle permanently out of reach.
