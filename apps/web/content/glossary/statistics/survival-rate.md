The share of battles a player finished alive, which reads as caution or as staying useful, depending on the rest of the account.

Surviving is worth something: a vehicle that lives keeps spotting, keeps holding a flank, and takes its hit points out of the enemy's reach. High survival alongside high damage is what a strong player looks like.

On its own it says little. A player who never trades and never pushes survives often and wins rarely, so survival rate is read next to win rate and damage rather than alone.
