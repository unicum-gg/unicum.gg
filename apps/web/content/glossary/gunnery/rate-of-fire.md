How many shells a gun can fire in a minute, the reciprocal of its reload time.

Rate of fire is reload expressed the other way round: a gun reloading in ten seconds fires six rounds per minute. Multiply it by alpha and you have DPM, which is why the three numbers are never read in isolation.

On a magazine gun the figure is an average across the whole cycle, so it hides the burst: the gun does nothing for twenty seconds, then delivers four shells in six.
