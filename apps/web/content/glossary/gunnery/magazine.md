A set of shells a gun can fire in quick succession before a long reload, the mechanism behind autoloaders and autoreloaders.

A magazine gun holds several shells at once. It fires them one after another separated only by the intra-clip reload, then stops for a full magazine reload that can run to thirty seconds or more.

This turns the gun into burst damage. A four-shell magazine at 390 alpha can remove 1,560 hit points in ten seconds, enough to delete most opponents outright, at the price of being defenceless afterwards.

Clip damage, the magazine's total, is the number that actually matters when deciding whether a trade is survivable.
