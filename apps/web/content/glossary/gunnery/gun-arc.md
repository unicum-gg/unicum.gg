The horizontal span a gun can cover without turning the hull, on vehicles that have no rotating turret.

A casemate vehicle mounts its gun in the hull with limited side-to-side travel, quoted as an arc in degrees. Anything outside it requires turning the whole vehicle, which costs time and widens dispersion.

A narrow arc is the defining weakness of most tank destroyers: they are lethal down one line and defenceless against anything that gets beside them.
