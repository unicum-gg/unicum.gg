The separate damage value a shell applies to modules and crew members, which is what decides whether a hit tracks, breaks or wounds.

Modules and crew have their own hit points, and a shell carries its own damage figure against them, usually different from the damage it deals to the vehicle itself. A hit that penetrates near the engine deals its vehicle damage and separately chips the engine's health.

This is why a shell that fails to penetrate can still track a target: the impact applies module damage to the tracks even when the hull takes nothing.
