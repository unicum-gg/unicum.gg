How far above horizontal a gun can point, which matters for shooting uphill and at vehicles on higher ground.

Elevation is depression's counterpart, measured in degrees above level. Most vehicles have enough of it for ordinary terrain, and the ones that do not are visibly helpless against a target sitting above them.

It also caps how high artillery can lob and how steep a hill a vehicle can fight up.
