The blast area of a high explosive shell, which damages everything within it whether or not the shell penetrated.

High explosive shells and artillery rounds carry a blast radius. Damage falls off from the point of impact outward and is reduced by whatever armor stands between the blast and the vehicle, so a near miss deals a fraction of the shell's alpha.

Splash is what makes artillery useful against a target hiding behind a rock and what lets a large high explosive shell strip modules from a vehicle it could never penetrate directly.
