A magazine gun that refills one shell at a time, so it can fire a single shot and keep loading in the background.

An autoreloader replaces the all-or-nothing clip with a rolling one. Each shell reloads individually, and the shells reload faster when the magazine is emptier, so the vehicle can fire once and keep its remaining rounds ready.

It plays like a normal gun that can burst on demand. The decision each engagement poses is whether to spend the magazine now for a kill or hold shells and stay a threat.
