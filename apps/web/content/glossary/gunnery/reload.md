The time between one shot and the next being ready, or for a magazine gun the time to refill the whole magazine.

Reload is the clock every gunnery decision runs on. It sets DPM, it sets how long a vehicle is exposed after firing, and it decides whether a trade is favourable: a shot fired against a gun that reloads in six seconds when yours takes twelve is a losing exchange even if your shell hits harder.

The loader's training, a rammer and a ventilation bonus all shorten it, and a damaged or injured loader lengthens it considerably.
