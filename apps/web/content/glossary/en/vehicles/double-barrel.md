---
term: Double-barrelled gun
aliases:
  - double barrel
  - twin gun
  - salvo
related:

---

A gun system with two barrels that can fire one shell at a time or both together in a salvo.

Double-barrelled vehicles load two shells and choose how to spend them: alternating single shots for steady damage, or a salvo that fires both at once for double alpha.

A salvo blocks the gun for a long cooldown afterwards, so it is the same trade an autoloader makes, compressed into one decision per reload.
