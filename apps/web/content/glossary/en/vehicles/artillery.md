The indirect fire class, firing high explosive from an overhead view at targets it cannot see directly.

Artillery aims from a top-down view and lobs shells over terrain, which lets it hit vehicles that are safe from everything else: hull down heavies, campers behind rocks, anything stationary.

Its shells stun as well as damage, slowing a target's reload and traverse for a few seconds, which is what makes an artillery hit felt long after the hit points are gone. It is the most argued-about class in the game precisely because it ignores positioning, the skill everything else rewards.
