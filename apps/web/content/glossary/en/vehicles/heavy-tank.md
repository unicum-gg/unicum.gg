The armored class: slow, tough and built to hold ground by absorbing shells other vehicles could not survive.

A heavy tank's job is the corner nobody else can hold. Its hit points and armor let it contest a chokepoint directly, and blocked damage is as much a measure of it doing its job as damage dealt.

Its weakness is committing to the wrong side of the map: a heavy that starts in the wrong place cannot get back, and by the time it arrives the flank it left has already lost.
