A high explosive variant with much higher nominal penetration, which behaves like HE when it fails to penetrate.

HESH keeps the explosive behaviour of HE and adds enough penetration to punch through moderate armor outright, where it deals full damage rather than a reduced blast amount.

Against thick or well-angled armor it falls back to being an expensive HE round, and against spaced armor it is stopped like any other explosive shell, so it is a situational premium round rather than a universal one.
