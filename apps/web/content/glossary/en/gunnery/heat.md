A shaped-charge shell with high penetration that ignores distance entirely, but is defeated by tracks, screens and any spaced armor it detonates on.

HEAT penetrates with a jet formed on impact rather than with kinetic energy. It therefore loses no penetration over distance and only ricochets beyond 85 degrees, which makes it the most reliable answer to heavily armored targets at range.

Its weakness is anything in front of the armor. The charge detonates on the first surface it meets, so tracks, side skirts, spaced plates and even a fence spend part of its penetration before it reaches the hull, and it cannot overmatch thin plates the way a solid shell does.

It gets no normalization at all, so a steeply sloped plate is measured at its full effective thickness.
