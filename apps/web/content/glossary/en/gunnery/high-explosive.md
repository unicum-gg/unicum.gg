A shell that explodes on contact, dealing reduced damage through armor it cannot penetrate and splashing everything nearby.

HE is the shell that always does something. If it penetrates, it deals full damage. If it does not, it still applies a reduced amount based on its blast, cut down by the armor thickness in the way, and it damages modules and crew through plating it could never defeat.

That makes it the answer to unarmored targets, to resetting a base capture, and to finishing a target with a few hit points left behind cover. Large-caliber low-velocity guns firing it are what players call derp guns.

Artillery fires a version of it exclusively, which is why an artillery hit hurts even when it lands beside a vehicle rather than on it.
