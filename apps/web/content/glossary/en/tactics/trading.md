Accepting a hit in order to land one, which is favourable or not depending on alpha, reload and remaining hit points.

Every exchange in the open is a trade. It pays when your shell is worth more than theirs, when your reload is shorter, or when your armor may bounce and theirs will not.

This is where alpha damage earns its reputation: a gun that removes 750 hit points per shot trades profitably against one that removes 320 even if the second has better damage per minute on paper.
