A position on an Onslaught map that a team captures to unlock a tactical skill, rather than to win the battle.

They are not bases. Capturing one wins nothing on its own: it hands your team an ability, on a cooldown, for as long as you hold the point. That is the difference that makes Onslaught play the way it does, because a map has a handful of them and both teams want the same ones, so the fights happen where the points are instead of where a base would be.

Each kind of point gives a different skill, and a map's set is fixed: the standard versions place an Artillery Headquarters and a Comms Center, while the night versions swap the Comms Center for an Observation Post. The minimap shows every point with its own icon, and which side currently holds it.
