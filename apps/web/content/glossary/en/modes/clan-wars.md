The campaign layer where clans fight for territory on a global map, with landing tournaments, province income and reward vehicles at the end of a season.

Clan Wars is played on a map of provinces. A clan lands through a tournament, holds ground against attacks in scheduled battles, and collects the province's income for as long as it keeps it. Battles are fifteen against fifteen at the campaign's tier, on a restricted map pool.

The commitment is the point: attacks happen at fixed hours, rosters have to be filled on time, and a territory is lost the moment a clan cannot field a team. This is the most organised thing in the game and the source of most reward vehicles in circulation.
