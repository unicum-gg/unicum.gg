The short Stronghold battle format, fought seven against seven or ten against ten at Tier VI, VIII or X for industrial resource.

Skirmishes run in scheduled windows through the day. A clan queues a team, plays a series of short battles, and converts the results into industrial resource for its Stronghold.

The small team size is what makes them a training ground: every vehicle matters, calls are heard, and the same seven players improve together in a way a random battle never asks for.
