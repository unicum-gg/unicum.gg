A battle royale mode fought in special upgradable vehicles on a shrinking map, where placement matters as much as damage.

Steel Hunter drops players solo or in two-player platoons onto a large map with a weak version of one of eight dedicated vehicles, one per nation. Loot and damage upgrade it from rank I to rank VII during the battle, danger zones shrink the playable area, and the last survivor wins.

**No statistics exist for it, here or anywhere.** Wargaming publishes no Steel Hunter block in any of its public interfaces: the mode is absent from the per-account statistics, and its eight vehicles carry zero battles in every per-vehicle mode breakdown the API serves, which is why this entry has numbers for the mode's rules and none for its players. A ranking cannot be built from nothing, and the one this site published under the name until October 2026 was reading a different mode's record entirely. See Rampage.
