A thirty against thirty Tier VIII mode on a nine square kilometre map, with respawns, capture zones and a rank earned per battle.

Frontline is the large-scale mode. Attackers push through lines of capture zones, defenders hold them, and both sides respawn when destroyed, so a battle runs long and a single loss is not the end of it.

Because it is Tier VIII only and pays well, it is the mode players use to grind credits, and its scoring rewards steady contribution across a long battle rather than one decisive engagement.
