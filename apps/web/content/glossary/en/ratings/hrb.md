The Steel Hunter rating with battle volume rewarded instead of discounted, for the players who keep winning a mode that is hard to win at all.

HR applies a volume discount, so a short lucky run cannot top the board. HRB replaces that brake with a term that grows with the battles played, on the reasoning that a top-five finish in a battle royale is rare enough that doing it often is the achievement.

It is rescaled so its median lands near HR's, which is what lets the two columns sit side by side and be read against the same colours.
