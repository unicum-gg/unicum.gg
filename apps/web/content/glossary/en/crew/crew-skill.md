An ability trained past 100 percent proficiency, which either improves gradually as it is learned or activates all at once when complete.

Once a crew member reaches full training in their role, further experience goes into skills. A skill applies progressively as it trains, like Repairs shortening module repair time. A perk does nothing until it hits 100 percent and then works fully, like Sixth Sense.

The choice of skills changes what a vehicle is. Concealment skills on a scout, gun handling skills on a sniper, repair speed on a heavy that expects to be tracked: the same vehicle with a differently trained crew plays differently.

Skills can be reset and retrained, at a cost, so a crew is not permanently locked into an early choice.
