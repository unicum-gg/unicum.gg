Moving a crew to a different vehicle, at a cost in training percentage unless gold is paid.

A crew trained on one vehicle loses proficiency when moved to another. Retraining with credits recovers part of it, gold recovers all of it, and doing nothing leaves the crew at a penalty that shows in every stat it governs.

Premium vehicles of the same nation are the exception: a crew moved onto one keeps its training, which is what makes them crew trainers.
