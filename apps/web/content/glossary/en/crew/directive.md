A consumable that boosts a piece of equipment or a crew skill for one battle.

Directives sit alongside equipment. One raises what a fitted item does, another improves a specific crew skill, and a few add abilities a vehicle would not otherwise have, such as a wet ammo rack that lowers detonation risk.

They are spent per battle, so they are used when the battle matters: in a clan format or on a vehicle being pushed for a third mark, rather than in every random battle.
