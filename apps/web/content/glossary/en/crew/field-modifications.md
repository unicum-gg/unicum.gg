Per-vehicle upgrades unlocked after a vehicle is elite, trading one stat against another in a series of choices.

Once a vehicle is fully researched and elite, field modifications open up. Each level offers a pair of options, better concealment or better view range, more penetration or more shell velocity, and the choice is exclusive.

They let a player specialise a vehicle toward how they actually use it, and they can be reset. They are available from Tier VI upward.
