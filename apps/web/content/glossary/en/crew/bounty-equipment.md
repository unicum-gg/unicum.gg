Stronger versions of standard equipment, earned with bonds or from events rather than bought with credits.

Improved equipment is bought with bonds, bounty equipment comes from events, and both do the same job as the standard item with a larger effect. A bounty rammer takes more off the reload than the credit one.

They are also the reason bonds matter: the difference between a standard and an improved loadout is small per item and noticeable across three slots.
