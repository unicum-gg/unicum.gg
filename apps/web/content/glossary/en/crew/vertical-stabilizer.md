The equipment that cuts the dispersion penalties for moving and traversing, turning a gun that must stop into one that can shoot on the move.

A stabiliser reduces every bloom multiplier by about a fifth. The base accuracy is untouched, so the specification sheet barely changes, and the vehicle nonetheless becomes a different thing to play.

It is worth most on vehicles that fight while repositioning and least on a tank destroyer that never moves before firing, which is the usual argument for putting optics or a camouflage net in that slot instead.
