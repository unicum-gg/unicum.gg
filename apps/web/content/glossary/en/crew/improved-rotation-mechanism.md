The equipment that speeds up hull and turret traverse and softens the dispersion penalty for turning.

It does two things at once: the vehicle turns faster, and turning costs less accuracy. On a heavy tank being circled, that combination is survival rather than convenience.

It is the usual alternative to a stabiliser on vehicles whose problem is not moving but turning.
