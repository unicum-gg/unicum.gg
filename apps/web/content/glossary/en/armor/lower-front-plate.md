term: Lower front plate

The strip of hull armor below the upper glacis, usually the flattest and thinnest part of a vehicle's front.

The upper front plate is normally thick and steeply sloped. The lower one sits closer to vertical because the vehicle has to drive over things, so it is where most frontal shots are aimed.

It is also the part a vehicle can hide. Parking behind a low obstacle, angling, or simply pointing the hull slightly away covers the lower plate and forces the shooter onto armor they may not defeat.
