A vehicle's health pool, which cannot be restored in battle and is therefore a resource to be spent rather than a buffer to be refilled.

Hit points are removed by every penetrating shell, by high explosive blasts, by fire and by ramming. Nothing puts them back: no consumable, no repair, no capture. Once spent they are gone for the rest of the battle.

That makes hit points a currency. A heavy tank trading two shots for three is spending health to buy space, and the reason blocking damage is valued so highly is that armor is the only thing that lets a vehicle contest ground without paying.

Higher tiers carry more, and within a tier the heavier classes carry the most, which is part of why bottom tier battles play so differently.
