The one, two or three marks painted on a vehicle's barrel, awarded for sustaining combined damage in the top 65, 85 or 95 percent of everyone playing that vehicle on the server.

Marks are a percentile, not a threshold. The game keeps a moving average of a player's combined damage on each vehicle, weighted toward recent battles, and compares it against the same figure for every other player of that vehicle on the region.

- One mark: better than 65 percent of players on that vehicle
- Two marks: better than 85 percent
- Three marks: better than 95 percent

Because the bar moves with the population, a mark is worth different damage on different servers and drifts over time. A vehicle that falls out of fashion gets easier to mark, a vehicle bought by everyone after a buff gets harder, and the exact damage needed today is what a stats site publishes.

The moving average is unforgiving in both directions: a run of weak battles pulls the percentage down again, so a third mark has to be held, not just reached.
