The share of shells fired that hit a target, distinct from a gun's accuracy value.

Hit ratio counts hits against shots fired. It reflects how a player shoots, waiting for the reticle to close, picking shots at range, not firing on the move, more than it reflects the gun.

It says nothing about penetration. A shell that hits and bounces counts as a hit, so a high hit ratio with low damage usually means a player is hitting armor rather than weak spots.
