---
term: Proximity spotting
aliases:

The rule that any vehicle within 50 metres is spotted regardless of concealment, as long as line of sight exists.

Inside 50 metres camouflage stops mattering. A vehicle in that radius is detected as soon as anything can see it, which is why driving past a bush line reveals whatever is hiding in it.

Scouts use this deliberately: a fast pass through a suspected position lights everything in it for the team, at the cost of being lit themselves.
