The credits deducted after a battle for repairing damage taken and replacing ammunition and consumables spent.

The bill is drawn from the battle: repairs scale with the damage the vehicle took, ammunition with what was fired, and premium shells and consumables are the expensive part of both.

This is what makes a fought battle cost more than a quiet one, and why the profitability of a vehicle is measured net of service rather than by the credits it earned.
