term: Demount kit
aliases:

The item that removes a piece of equipment from a vehicle without paying the gold fee.

Equipment can always be moved, at a cost: gold, or a demount kit earned from events and missions. Standard equipment can also be sold back for credits at a loss.

It is what makes one good loadout usable across several vehicles, which is how most players run improved equipment they own only one of.
