A currency earned from competitive play and high placements, spent on improved equipment, directives and reward vehicles.

Bonds come from finishing near the top of a random battle at high tier, from Ranked Battles, and from clan formats. They cannot be bought.

They buy the improved versions of equipment, the strongest directives, and a small selection of reward vehicles, which makes them the marker of consistent competitive play rather than of spending.
