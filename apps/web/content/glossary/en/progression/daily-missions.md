The rotating set of small objectives that reset each day, paying experience multipliers and credits.

Each day brings a handful of objectives at different difficulties, plus experience multipliers to spend on a good battle. Rerolling an objective is allowed a limited number of times.

The multipliers are the real reward: applied to a strong battle they are worth more than the mission itself.
