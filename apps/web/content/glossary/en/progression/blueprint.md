Fragments collected from battles that reduce the experience needed to research a specific vehicle, or any vehicle of a nation.

Blueprints come in three kinds: fragments for one specific vehicle, fragments for any vehicle of a nation, and universal fragments that work anywhere. Assembling a full blueprint removes a large share of the research cost, and a complete one removes all of it.

They are earned from battles at Tier II and above and from events, so they accumulate quietly and take the edge off the longest grinds.
