The screen the game returns to between battles, holding every vehicle an account owns.

The garage is where vehicles are bought, equipped, crewed and repaired, and where the battle queue is joined. Each vehicle takes a slot, and slots are bought with gold, which is the quiet limit on hoarding tanks.

Players say a vehicle is in their garage to mean they own it, and a garage slot is what a reward vehicle actually costs them.
