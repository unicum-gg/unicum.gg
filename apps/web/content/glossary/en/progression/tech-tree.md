A nation's chart of researchable vehicles, where each one unlocks the vehicles above it.

Every nation has a tree of branches running from Tier I up to Tier X, split by class. Researching a vehicle unlocks whatever sits above it, so reaching a specific Tier X means grinding the specific line that leads to it.

Branches share vehicles where the historical lines converged, which is why an unusual route through a tree is sometimes cheaper than the obvious one.
