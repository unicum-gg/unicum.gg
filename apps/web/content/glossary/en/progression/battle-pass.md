A season-long track of rewards unlocked by earning points on a chosen set of vehicles.

Each season runs for a few months. Points come from finishing near the top of a battle on vehicles the player nominates, and the track pays out bonds, crew books, equipment and, at the end, a 3D style or a reward vehicle.

It is the main reason to keep playing a small set of vehicles rather than spreading battles across a garage, and the improved version bought with gold adds a second track of rewards.
