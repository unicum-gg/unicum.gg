Events that may fire partway through a battle on a handful of Random Battle maps, changing the ground they strike for the rest of it.

An airship comes down over a square, a bombing run flattens a block, a train derails along its track. They are a chance rather than a fixture: two battles on the same map can play out with and without one, which is why the game says an event *might* occur here. The area is marked before it happens, with smoke on the map and an outline on the minimap, and a vehicle caught inside when the event lands can be destroyed by it. Once it has passed, that patch of the map is different for the rest of the battle: rubble closes a route, an aircraft carcass becomes hard cover, a collapsed wall opens a line that was not there at the start.

Only some maps carry them, and a map that does can carry more than one. They are optional: the Game tab of the settings has a switch that keeps you out of the matchmaker's random-event maps altogether.

They matter to a plan more than their size suggests, precisely because they are uncertain. A position that holds early is not the same position once an event has landed, and a route a push depends on can close while it is under way, so the ground worth committing to is the ground that survives either way.
