Organised bracket play between teams, run by Wargaming or by the community, outside the normal queues.

Tournaments run on a schedule with fixed team sizes and tiers, from open brackets anyone can enter to the invitational circuit at the top. Rewards are gold, bonds and reward vehicles.

They are the most demanding format in the game, and the training ground for the clans that hold territory on the global map.
