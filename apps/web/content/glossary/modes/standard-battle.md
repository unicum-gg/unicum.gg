The default objective: each team has its own base, and wins by capturing the other's or destroying every enemy vehicle.

Standard is the objective most maps default to. Two bases, one per team, fifteen minutes, and two routes to a win. Most battles end in destruction rather than capture, and the base mainly matters as a threat that forces a team to come back and defend.

Spawns are fixed per team, which is what makes each map's flanks and ridges predictable, and what map knowledge is really about.
