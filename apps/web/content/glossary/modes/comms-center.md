The Onslaught point of interest that spots enemy vehicles for the team holding it, wherever they are on the map.

It is the informational half of a map's points, and it answers the question every Onslaught battle turns on: where are the other seven. A team that holds it knows which flank is stacked before committing to it, which is worth more in a seven a side match than in a random battle, where one misread costs a fraction of the team.

The reveal lasts a set time and then goes on cooldown, so it is a snapshot rather than permanent vision. On the night versions of a map, an Observation Post takes its place.
