The fifteen against fifteen Tier X Stronghold format, where clans attack each other's bases in scheduled events for a larger share of resource.

Advances is the full-size Stronghold battle. Fifteen against fifteen on Tier X, run as an event over several days, with clans attacking and defending directly rather than queuing for a ladder.

It sits between a Skirmish and Clan Wars: the same fifteen-player commitment as a campaign, without holding territory on a map, and it is where clan strength is most visible over a short window.
