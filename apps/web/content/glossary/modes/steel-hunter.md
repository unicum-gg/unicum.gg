A battle royale mode fought in special upgradable vehicles on a shrinking map, where placement matters as much as damage.

Steel Hunter drops players solo or in small teams onto a large map with a weak version of a special vehicle. Loot upgrades it mid-battle, a closing storm shrinks the playable area, and the last survivor wins.

None of the usual statistics transfer. The vehicles exist only in this mode, damage is spread across a long game, and surviving to the final circle is worth more than any single fight, which is why it is rated on its own scale.
