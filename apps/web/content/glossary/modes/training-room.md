A private battle a player creates and invites others into, used for practice, testing and clan drills.

A training room is a battle with no matchmaker, no rewards and no consequences: the creator picks the map, the teams and the rules. It is where clans rehearse Clan Wars calls and where players measure things the interface will not tell them.

Nothing that happens in one is recorded on an account's statistics, which is exactly why it is useful for testing.
