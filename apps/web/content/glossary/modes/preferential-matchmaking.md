term: Preferential matchmaking
aliases:

---

A narrower battle-tier spread on some vehicles, so they never meet opponents two tiers above them.

A handful of vehicles, nearly all of them premium, see only one tier above their own. A Tier VIII with preferential matchmaking meets Tier VIII and IX, never X.

It is a real advantage and a real trade: those vehicles are usually weaker than their peers precisely because the narrower spread was priced in.
