Damage dealt to enemies standing in your own base, credited as defence points and measured by how much capture it wiped out.

A vehicle capturing a base loses the points it personally accumulated the moment it is hit. Whoever landed that shot is credited with the capture it undid, which is the statistic the account carries as defence.

It is the cheapest way a player can decide a battle: a single shot into the circle can erase a minute of three vehicles capturing, and the counter is the only trace of it in the ledger.
