A thirty against thirty Tier X format on oversized maps, mixed into the random queue rather than run as its own mode.

Grand Battles use maps roughly four times the area of a standard one and double the team size. There are no respawns: it is a random battle at a larger scale, with two bases and a longer clock.

The scale changes the vehicles that work. Distances favour view range and gun handling over brawling armor, and a flank that collapses takes much longer to punish.
