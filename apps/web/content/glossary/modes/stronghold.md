A clan's own base, built from buildings that produce industrial resource and unlock reserves, and the battles fought to supply it.

The Stronghold is a clan's persistent structure. Its buildings are levelled with industrial resource earned in Skirmishes and Advances, and in return they unlock reserves, temporary bonuses to experience, credits or crew training that a commander activates for everyone online.

It gives a clan a reason to play together outside of a campaign, and it is the ladder most clans are actually ranked on day to day.
