Making an enemy vehicle visible to your whole team, which is what turns a position into a target.

Nothing can be shot at until it is spotted. The game runs vision checks between the spotting vehicle's viewpoints and points on the target: if a line of sight exists and the distance is inside the target's detection range, the target is revealed to the entire team.

A spot lasts while the check keeps succeeding, plus a few seconds of grace after it stops, which is the window in which a vehicle that has broken line of sight is still shootable.

Damage dealt to a target you spotted is credited to you as assistance, which is how the contribution of a scout is measured at all.
