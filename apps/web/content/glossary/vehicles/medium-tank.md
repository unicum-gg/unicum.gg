The flexible class: enough speed to take position, enough gun to trade, enough armor to survive a mistake.

Mediums do a bit of everything, which is why they decide most battles. They arrive at a flank before the heavies, they spot when no light tank is left, and they finish what artillery and tank destroyers soften.

The class covers the widest range of designs, from fast flankers with weak guns to slow, well-armored support vehicles that fight like heavy tanks.
