A light vehicle on wheels rather than tracks, with a high-speed mode, a different damage model and no tracks to break.

Wheeled vehicles trade the ability to turn on the spot for enormous speed. They have two driving modes, a manoeuvrable one and a fast one that raises top speed while restricting steering and the gun.

Their wheels are damaged individually instead of a track being broken, so they are hard to stop outright, and their speed makes them the fastest way to spot a flank and the hardest thing in the game to hit.
