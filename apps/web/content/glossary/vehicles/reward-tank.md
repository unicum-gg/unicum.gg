A vehicle that cannot be bought, given out for personal missions, campaigns, Clan Wars or bonds.

Reward vehicles are earned. Some come from personal mission campaigns, some from Clan Wars seasons, some from the bond shop, and a few from events that never returned.

They usually behave like premium vehicles economically, and their real value is that they mark what a player has done: a Clan Wars reward vehicle in a garage is a season of organised play.
