---
term: Lemming train
aliases:
  - lemming
  - lemming rush
related:
  - minimap
  - flanking
  - camping
---

Most of a team driving to the same flank at once, leaving the rest of the map undefended.

A lemming train wins its flank overwhelmingly and loses the battle, because the two or three vehicles left elsewhere cannot hold, and the base falls or the team is caught from behind before the push arrives.

It happens because players follow the vehicle in front of them at the start, and the only reliable counter is to look at the minimap in the first ten seconds and go where nobody else is.
