The mod that popularised showing every player's rating in the battle loading screen, and the origin of the colour scale everyone uses.

XVM reads public statistics and paints the loading screen with each player's rating colour, along with a predicted chance of winning. The colour bands it chose became the community standard, which is why a stats site and a mod agree on what purple means.

It is also the reason showing ratings in battle is controversial: seeing the colours before the first shot changes how players treat their own team.
