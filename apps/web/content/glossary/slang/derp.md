A large-caliber, low-velocity gun firing high explosive, which deals damage to almost anything without needing to penetrate.

A derp gun trades accuracy, aim time and rate of fire for an enormous shell. It rarely penetrates properly armored targets, and it does not need to: the high explosive blast deals partial damage anyway and strips modules and crew through the plating.

It is the great equaliser at low and mid tiers, where a Tier V howitzer can remove a third of a Tier VII's health with a lucky roll.
