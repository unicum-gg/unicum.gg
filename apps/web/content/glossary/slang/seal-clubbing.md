An experienced player farming low tiers, where the opposition is mostly people still learning the game.

Low tiers are where new accounts live. A veteran taking a fully equipped Tier III with a trained crew into that queue wins effortlessly, and the statistics that come out of it look extraordinary for reasons that have nothing to do with difficulty.

It is why a very high win rate at low tier is discounted when reading an account, and why per-vehicle ratings compare a player against others in the same vehicle rather than against the server as a whole.
