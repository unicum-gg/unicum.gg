The commander perk that lights a bulb a few seconds after the vehicle has been spotted, the single most valuable ability in the game.

Sixth Sense tells a player the one thing the interface otherwise hides: that the enemy can see them. The indicator appears a short delay after the spot begins, and from that moment the player knows a shell may already be on its way.

It changes every decision. A scout knows to move, a heavy knows to angle, a tank destroyer knows its bush has been found. Playing without it is playing blind, which is why it is trained first on nearly every commander in the game.
