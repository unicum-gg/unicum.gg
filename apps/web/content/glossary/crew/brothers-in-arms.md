A perk that raises every crew member's effectiveness, but only while the entire crew has it trained.

Brothers in Arms adds a flat bonus to the major qualification of every crew member, which flows through into view range, reload, aim time and handling at once. It is the closest thing to a free upgrade a vehicle can get.

The catch is that it is all or nothing: a single crew member without it, or a crew member who was replaced, switches it off for everyone. It also stacks with ventilation and a food consumable, which is why those three appear together on most well-built vehicles.
