A single-use item carried into battle: repair kits, first aid kits, fire extinguishers and the food that boosts the whole crew.

Consumables are the emergency toolkit. A repair kit fixes a broken module, a first aid kit heals a wounded crew member, an extinguisher stops a fire. Their improved versions can be used more than once and work automatically.

The fourth slot is usually a food or drink item, which raises every crew member's effectiveness for the whole battle, at the price of being lost if the vehicle catches fire and of costing credits every time it is spent.
