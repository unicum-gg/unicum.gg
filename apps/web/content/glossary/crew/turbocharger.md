The equipment that adds engine power and top speed, the standard fix for a vehicle that arrives late.

A turbocharger lifts both the top speed and the power behind it, so the vehicle accelerates harder and holds more speed across soft ground. On a medium that has to reach a position first, it is often worth more than any gun equipment.

It is the mobility slot's default, in the same way a rammer is the firepower slot's.
