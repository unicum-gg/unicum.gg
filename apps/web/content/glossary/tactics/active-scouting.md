---
term: Active scouting
aliases:
  - active spotting

related:

---

Driving fast through or past enemy positions to reveal them, accepting exposure in exchange for information.

An active scout uses speed and proximity spotting to light a position that nothing can see from safety. Done at the right moment it hands the team every target on a flank at once.

Done at the wrong moment, which is most of the time in the first minute, it costs the team a vehicle for a few seconds of vision. The difference is timing: after the enemy has committed, an active pass is information, before it, it is a donation.
