---
term: Kiting
aliases:
  - circling
  - reverse kiting
related:
  - turret-traverse
  - hull-traverse
  - top-speed
  - view-range
---

Staying at a distance an opponent cannot close, retreating as they advance and firing on the way.

A faster vehicle with a usable gun can fight a slower one indefinitely by never letting it into range. Each time the opponent commits, the kiter backs off, fires, and repeats.

Circling is the close-range version: driving around a slower vehicle faster than its turret can follow. Both depend on knowing the opponent's traverse speeds, and both end badly if a second enemy is watching.
