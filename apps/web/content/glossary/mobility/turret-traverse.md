How fast the turret rotates, in degrees per second, independent of the ground the vehicle is standing on.

Turret traverse decides how quickly a gun can be brought onto a target that appears from an unexpected direction, and whether a vehicle can track a fast opponent without turning its hull.

It comes with a cost: turning the turret widens the aiming circle, so a slow turret and heavy turret bloom together make a gun that is always a moment behind.
