A paid subscription that multiplies the credits and experience earned in every battle.

A premium account raises credit and experience income by a set percentage for as long as it is active. It changes nothing about a vehicle's performance, only what the battle pays.

It is what makes high-tier play sustainable: the same Tier X battle that loses credits on a standard account roughly breaks even with premium time running.
