The purchased currency, spent on premium time, premium vehicles, converting experience and retraining crews.

Gold is bought with money and, for clans, earned from Clan Wars territory income. It buys premium time and premium vehicles, converts vehicle experience into free experience, retrains a crew without loss and expands the garage.

It no longer buys ammunition. Premium shells were moved to credits years ago, though the community still calls them gold rounds.
