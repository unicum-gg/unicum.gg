A timed multiplier on credits, experience or crew training, activated by a player or by a clan for everyone online.

Boosters run for a set duration once activated, multiplying one kind of income. They stack with a premium account, so a session planned around them earns far more than the same battles played cold.

Clans have their own version: Stronghold reserves, which a commander activates for every member currently in the game, which is one of the practical reasons to be in an active clan.
