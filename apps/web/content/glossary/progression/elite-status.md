The state of a vehicle whose modules and successors are all researched, which unlocks crew training and experience conversion.

Once everything a vehicle leads to is researched, it becomes elite. Its experience can then be redirected into accelerated crew training or converted into free experience with gold, instead of accumulating with nothing to buy.

Elite status is also what opens field modifications, so it is the point at which a vehicle stops being a step in a grind and becomes something a player keeps.
