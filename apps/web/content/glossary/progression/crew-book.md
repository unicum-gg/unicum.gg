An item that grants a lump of crew experience, the fastest way to bring a crew up to its next skill.

Crew books come in national and universal forms and are earned from missions, campaigns and the battle pass. Using one adds experience directly to a crew, which is worth several hundred battles of training on a fresh vehicle.

They are the practical answer to the stock-and-untrained-crew stretch that makes a new Tier X miserable.
