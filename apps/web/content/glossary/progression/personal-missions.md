Long campaigns of specific in-battle objectives whose completion awards reward vehicles, crews and equipment.

Each mission asks for something precise: block a set amount of damage, spot for a target, survive and win. They are grouped into campaigns by class, and each has a harder secondary condition attached.

They are the main solo route to reward vehicles and to a female crew with extra skills, and they are deliberately demanding enough that finishing a campaign is a marker of a serious player.
