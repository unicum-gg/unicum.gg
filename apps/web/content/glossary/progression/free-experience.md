Experience that can be spent on any vehicle or module, accumulated as a small share of everything earned and convertible from elite vehicles with gold.

A small percentage of the experience earned in every battle is set aside as free experience. It is not tied to a vehicle, so it can be spent to skip a stock configuration, unlock a module outright, or jump ahead in a tree.

Experience sitting on an elite vehicle can also be converted into free experience with gold, which is how players who have finished a line turn a dormant balance into progress elsewhere.
