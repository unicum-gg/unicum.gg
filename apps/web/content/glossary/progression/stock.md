A vehicle in its starting configuration, before any module has been researched, which is usually much weaker than its final form.

A stock vehicle carries the previous tier's gun on a chassis that cannot support anything better, and it fights vehicles two tiers above it. The gap between stock and fully upgraded is large enough that the two configurations are effectively different vehicles.

The usual remedies are free experience to skip the worst module, keeping a well trained crew, or accepting a difficult stretch of battles. Players call it the stock grind.
